Before You Give an AI Agent the Keys

AI agents can edit files, update records, send messages, use tools, and carry work across multiple steps. That creates capacity, but it also creates an authority question: what may the agent do, what requires approval, and how will you know what happened afterward?

  • Define the agent's authority before it acts.
  • Make permissions explicit for sending, spending, publishing, deleting, and changing records.
  • Keep clear human decision points without creating approval fatigue.
  • Treat access and governance as different design decisions.
  • Keep enough operating evidence to reconstruct what happened.
